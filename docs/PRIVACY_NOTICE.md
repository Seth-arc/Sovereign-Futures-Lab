# Workshop privacy notice

Sovereign collects your name, organization, email address, workshop decisions, written rationales, your next institutional handoff, evidence requests, activity timestamps, messages, AI-advisor transcripts, and transfer reflection to operate and debrief the **A Data-Informed Simulation for African Foresight Practice** workshop in the Futures Lab experience. The next institutional handoff is retained with the current decision record and with every versioned negotiation-preparation brief you submit. The transfer response continues to use the existing reflection field and does not alter a submitted recommendation snapshot.

Microphone audio is sent to the configured transcription provider only when you press and hold the voice control. Audio is not stored by this application after transcription. You can review and edit the transcript before submitting it to an advisor.

The workshop facilitator can review individual activity and detailed after-action reports. Participants receive only their own debrief reconstruction: submitted version, evidence available at submission, bounded exercise consequences and unresolved risks, one fixed-assumption counterfactual, relevant facilitator updates, and transfer reflection. Participants do not receive another participant's identity, decision, transcript, or detailed after-action report. During the group debrief, the facilitator can use an anonymized comparison view.

Workshop data is used for training delivery, not research or individual performance scoring. It is retained for up to 30 days after the workshop and may be deleted earlier by the facilitator. Identified CSV and report exports are restricted to the facilitator.

By joining the workshop, you confirm that you understand this use of your information. If you do not consent, do not enter the workshop and speak with the facilitator.
