# Workshop privacy notice

Consent version `2026-10-13` covers the processing described here. Sovereign retains your name, organization, and email; decisions, rationales, next institutional handoff, and versioned submissions; evidence requests, activity timestamps, facilitator updates, and institutional messages; advisor questions, answers, citations, and transfer reflection; and bounded replay metadata used to reconstruct a submitted version. Replay metadata contains version identifiers, bounded evidence-availability timestamps, and relevant facilitator-update and answered-message IDs rather than source documents or conversation bodies. The next institutional handoff is retained with the current decision record and every versioned negotiation-preparation brief. The transfer reflection does not alter a submitted recommendation snapshot.

Microphone audio is sent to the configured external AI transcription provider only after you activate recording by pressing and holding the voice control. Audio is not stored by this application after transcription. You can review and edit the transcript before submitting it to an advisor.

When you ask an advisor, the advisor question, recent advisor conversation, participant-visible scenario and evidence context, filtered working decisions, visible facilitator updates, answered institutional messages, and selected bounded research-card fields may be sent to the configured external AI provider. Selected card fields are limited to bounded claims, short verified excerpts, scope conditions, prohibited inferences, and citation metadata. Source files and full reports are not sent to the provider.

Identified records and exports are available to the workshop facilitator for delivery and debrief, including individual activity and detailed after-action reports. Participants receive only their own debrief reconstruction: submitted version, evidence available at submission, bounded exercise consequences and unresolved risks, one fixed-assumption counterfactual, relevant facilitator updates, and transfer reflection. Participants do not receive another participant's identity, decision, transcript, or detailed after-action report. During the group debrief, the facilitator can use an anonymized comparison view.

Workshop data is used for training delivery, not research or individual performance scoring. Application records are retained for up to 30 days after the workshop and may be deleted earlier by the facilitator. This application-retention period does not promise a deletion schedule for provider systems or backups; those are governed by the selected provider terms. Identified CSV and report exports are restricted to the facilitator.

Your cloud participant record remains linked to the anonymous sign-in session in the browser where you joined. Refreshing that browser can resume the same record. Entering the same workshop code and email in another browser does not transfer or reveal the record; return to the original browser or contact the facilitator. Cross-device recovery is not currently supported.

By joining the workshop, you confirm that you understand this use of your information. If you do not consent, do not enter the workshop and speak with the facilitator.
