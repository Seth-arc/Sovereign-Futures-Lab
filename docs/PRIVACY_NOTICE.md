# Workshop privacy notice

Sovereign collects your name, organization, email address, workshop decisions, written rationales, evidence requests, activity timestamps, messages, AI-advisor transcripts, and reflection to operate and debrief the **A Data-Informed Simulation for African Foresight Practice** workshop in the Futures Lab experience.

Microphone audio is sent to the configured transcription provider only when you press and hold the voice control. Audio is not stored by this application after transcription. You can review and edit the transcript before submitting it to an advisor.

The workshop facilitator can review individual activity and detailed after-action reports. Participants receive only the participant reflection view. During the group debrief, the facilitator can use an anonymized comparison view.

Workshop data is used for training delivery, not research or individual performance scoring. It is retained for up to 30 days after the workshop and may be deleted earlier by the facilitator. Identified CSV and report exports are restricted to the facilitator.

By joining the workshop, you confirm that you understand this use of your information. If you do not consent, do not enter the workshop and speak with the facilitator.
